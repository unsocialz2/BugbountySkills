Status meaning:
- `lead` — hypothesis/signal only.
- `candidate` — partial evidence, still needs falsification or reach/impact proof.
- `validated` — evidence contract + falsification passed.
- `reported` — submitted/disclosed.
- `closed` — disproven, expected behavior, OOS, duplicate-without-new-angle, or otherwise not actionable.

Every promotion or closure needs a reason and decisive evidence reference. Configuration, response acceptance, demonstrated execution, and state change are separate claims. Missing evidence keeps a claim unverified; stopping for access/cost is a parked hypothesis rather than proof of safety.

Keep secrets/PII redacted. Detailed raw evidence can live in `recon_data/` or a dedicated finding folder; link it here rather than pasting large blobs.
